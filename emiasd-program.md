## About the program

**[Executive Master Intelligence Artificielle & Science des Données](https://executive-education.dauphine.psl.eu/formations/executive-master-diplome-universite/ia-science-donnees)** — Dauphine Executive Education, Université Paris-Dauphine \| PSL.

A part-time program designed for working professionals, awarding a *Diplôme d'Université* (DU) at Bac+5 level.

- **Duration:** 340 hours over 18 months
- **Format:** Monday evenings (18:00–21:00), one full Tuesday per month (08:30–18:30), plus four intensive three-day blocks
- **Location:** PariSanté Campus, Paris 15th
- **Admission:** professionals with solid IT skills and an applied mathematics background; Bac+3/5 in computer science, engineering, or equivalent

## Curriculum

The program balances two core domains:

**1. Data management & Big Data**
- Extraction, transformation and aggregation of massive datasets
- Unstructured data handling
- Parallel and distributed processing

**2. Machine Learning & AI**
- Machine learning algorithms
- Deep learning applications
- Generative AI and large language models (LLMs)
- Agentic AI systems
- Real-time data stream processing

**Technical skills covered:** Python, Scala, Hadoop, Spark, mathematical foundations, algorithmic analysis.

Teaching combines theoretical foundations with hands-on implementation, delivered by instructors who are recognized specialists and researchers in their fields, with content that evolves alongside current research and industry practice.

## Career outcomes

Graduates typically move into roles such as Data Scientist, Data Engineer, ML Engineer, AI Consultant, Data Analyst, or AI Entrepreneur.
